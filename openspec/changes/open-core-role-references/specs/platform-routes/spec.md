## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Route role references open their Core role details

The Core-direct route Roles tab SHALL offer Open in a new tab for each displayed role, following `core-role-reference-navigation`, without changing `userRoles` membership behavior.

#### Scenario: Open a route role

- **WHEN** a user selects Open in a new tab on a route role row
- **THEN** the unqualified platform-role detail address for that role opens in a new tab
